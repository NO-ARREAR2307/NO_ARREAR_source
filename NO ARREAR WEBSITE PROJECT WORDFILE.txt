Things I noticed

The zip includes node_modules (~146 MB) and three uploaded papers. The node_modules has Windows-only native binaries (sharp-win32-x64, @napi-rs/canvas-win32), so it won't run on Linux or macOS without a reinstall. The papers in uploads/papers/ look like real user files, which your .gitignore is meant to keep out of version control.
No per-user or stricter rate limit on /auth/login, /auth/register or the /questions/ask* endpoints. Only the global 300 requests per 15 minutes per IP applies. That leaves room for brute-force attempts and for running up your Gemini quota.
The Subject model exists but has no routes or controllers.
Papers can't be listed, fetched or deleted through the API. Questions and answers also aren't saved anywhere, and Q&A isn't connected to extracted paper text yet.
Smaller points:
Tokens last 7 days with no revocation.
Up to 1M characters of extracted text sit in a single MongoDB document.
trust proxy is only set in production.
The default Gemini model name, gemini-3.8-flash, isn't something I've verified.
